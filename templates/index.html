Set up your monitor

Pair your monitor with this computer once. After that, syncing is one button.

Sync readings

  1. With the monitor off, press the Connection button on its back once. Don't hold it.
  2. When the transfer symbol flashes, press Sync. If you see a flashing P instead, that's pairing mode: press START/STOP and try again.
